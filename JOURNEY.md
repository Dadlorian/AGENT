Origin owner 2026-09-08: frame the platform as a customer journey of five, seven or nine single-letter steps, left to right, covering the whole end to end. Each step exposes the domains, entities and standards it needs, so anyone can say where they are. Customers walk it, and so do agents. Taken further here (proposed): every column is computed from the registry, the skills, the ontology, the examples and the litmus scorecard, so the journey doubles as the gap map.

```
A Ask  →  B Bound  →  C Contain  →  D Do  →  E Evidence  →  F Finish  →  G Grow
```

## Actors

- **person**: the customer: an engineer, operator or reviewer who hands work in and lives with the result
- **agent**: a unit of agent work, or an external agent at the fourth door, walking the same steps from the inside

## The steps

Read top to bottom in letter order; that is the left-to-right walk. Each later table adds one layer to the same seven rows.

| Step | What happens here | The person asks | The agent asks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A Ask** | Intent from any of the four doors becomes one validated envelope with an actor, a key and a correlation id, and the caller gets an acknowledgement at once. | I have intent. How do I hand it over from where I am, and what do I get back at once? | Which door am I entering by, who am I acting for, and what is my envelope? |
| **B Bound** | The document is priced, its ceilings set, its policy decided and its schedule computed before anything is spent or any cell exists. | What will this cost, what is it allowed to touch, and when does it run? | What is my ceiling, which rule decided I may act, and what schedule fired me? |
| **C Contain** | A cell is provisioned to the declared security profile, the contract and workspace are mounted, tools are bound and the unit carries an attested identity. | Where does it run, what can it reach, and who says so? | What is my cell, what is mounted, which tools are bound, and what identity do I carry? |
| **D Do** | The harness runs one turn inside the cell under the ceiling: a model class answers, tools are called, and the turn ends with a typed stop reason or a typed failure. | What happens in the sandbox on my behalf, and what if fifty run at once? | One turn: what did I get, which model class answered, which tool ran, and why did I stop? |
| **E Evidence** | Everything the unit does leaves a correlated trail a person can watch from their own surface and steer by approving, editing, rejecting or answering. | How do I see it from where I am, and how do I intervene? | What am I emitting, who is watching, and what happens when I must ask a person? |
| **F Finish** | The work product is judged against visible and hidden checks, attested, recorded in the tamper-evident log, and given a disposition that moves it toward production. | How do I know it is finished, how does it reach production, and what happens next? | What did I produce, who judged it, what was attested, and what is my disposition? |
| **G Grow** | Usage, issues and outcomes feed the improvement loop, which revises templates and skills toward the target and proves each revision against the evaluation gate. | What did the platform learn, and what changed because of it? | Which skill or template shaped me, was it measured, and which revision replaced it? |

## How to use it

- Say where you are by letter. A pre-flight card, a STATUS row or a ceremony names the step it moves.
- A new standard enters the registry first, with a position and a reason, then the step that exposes it, then the owning skill's contract. A standard in no step is an error.
- At a phase boundary the improvement loop reads this page: the gaps column is the target, the litmus column is the measure.
