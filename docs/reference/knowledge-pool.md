# Knowledge pool — what stands behind each card

Generated by `python3 tools/knowledge_pool.py` from citations present in `docs/reference/cards/*.json`. Every number is a join against a real record id, not a score. Re-run it rather than trusting these figures later.

## What the grades mean

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **documented** | A profile exists and 3+ of its citations rest on a page someone actually fetched and read. |
| **unread** | A profile exists with real, verified citations — often 8 to 17 of them — but the research behind them is search-result snippets. Nobody opened the page. This is *not* an absence of work; it is work at a weaker evidence grade. |
| **undocumented** | No profile. Nothing has been written down for this card. |

**27 documented · 5 unread · 8 undocumented**, of 40 cards.

## Two bodies of research, and where each landed

- **direct** — 180 citations to research commissioned for the reference model (`X-refmodel-*` lenses, fetched pages)
- **prior** — 126 citations to the research corpus that existed before it (other `X-` lenses, search snippets)
- **repo** — 140 citations to this repo's own documents (`F-` facts, `T-` targets, `A-` architecture, `REF-` reference)

## By card

`cites` counts every verified citation. `fetch` is how many rest on a fetched page. `lens` is the size of that card's dedicated research lens, if it has one.
