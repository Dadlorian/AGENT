# Knowledge quality — what KIND of evidence stands behind the model

Generated by `python3 tools/knowledge_quality.py`. Re-run it rather than trusting these figures later; every one is a join against a real record, not a score.

`knowledge_pool.py` asks whether a card is covered. This asks whether its evidence is worth trusting, which is a different question and the one that decides whether examples can safely be built on it.

## Every quote, typed

Not whether a citation passed, but **what kind of difference** there is between the sentence a card asserts and the page it names. A bare true/false is what made this repo's own reporting wrong: it scored a flattened bullet list and an invented statistic identically, and the difference got supplied from imagination.

| Verdict | Meaning | Quotes | Share | Gate |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| `exact` | the quote is byte-identical on the page | 217 | 92.7% | passes |
| `formatting` | same words; markdown, a flattened list, line numbers or a pronoun differ | 7 | 3.0% | passes |
| `unretrievable` | the page could not be read - proves nothing either way | 6 | 2.6% | recorded |
| `partial` | part is on the page, part is not - **a person must read this one** | 3 | 1.3% | blocks until reviewed |
| `stitched` | two non-adjacent passages joined into one sentence | 1 | 0.4% | blocks |

**4 of 234 quotes need a human decision.** Everything else the system settled on its own. That number, not the percentage above, is the one to act on.

| Class | What it means | Citations | Share |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **read** | External page fetched, and the quoted snippet re-verified against the live page | 291 | 64.5% |
| **unread** | External source, page never opened — a search-result snippet | 17 | 3.8% |
| **internal** | This repo's own documents (F/T/REF/A) — intent or current state, not industry practice | 143 | 31.7% |
| **broken** | Cites an id that resolves nowhere in the knowledge base | 0 | 0.0% |

**451 citations across 32 card profiles.** 308 are external (68.3%); of those, 17 (5.5% of external) rest on a page nobody opened.

This repo measured 17 of 68 search-only records contradicting their own page. At that rate the 17 unread citations carry roughly **4 citations that their source does not support**.
